Research and annotations

KU-PCPE accompanies “Grounding Image Composition with Label-Conditioned Visual Evidence,” accepted at WACV 2027, by Armin Dadras, Tingyu Lin, Robert Sablatnig, Franziska Proksa, and Markus Seidl. Affiliations: Computer Vision Lab, TU Wien, and UAS St. Pölten.

The KU-PCPE annotation layer is licensed under CC BY 4.0. The independent evaluator is MIT licensed. The repository provides one dataset download and separate assets for reproducing the paper’s results.

Example photographs and masks

The explorer uses exactly the 18 typical and ambiguous tasks selected for the paper. The photographs are resized from KU-PCP; their rights remain with their original owners and are not covered by the website code license or the annotation license. Original KU-PCP images must be obtained separately under the distributor’s terms.

Blue overlays show each expert’s final mask and the two masks derived by consensus. One consensus uses a strict majority; the other first accounts for complementary selections. All scores come from the verified results in the paper. The accompanying provenance file records which examples were used and the hashes of their source files.

Original dataset reference: Jun-Tae Lee, Han-Ul Kim, Chul Lee, and Chang-Su Kim. Photographic composition classification and dominant geometric element detection for outdoor scenes. Journal of Visual Communication and Image Representation 55:91–105, 2018.
