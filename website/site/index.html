Overview

Composition labels describe an image as a whole, but do not identify the regions that support the judgment. KU-PCP Elements extends KU-PCP by asking people to mark these regions for nine composition classes. One annotator supplies masks for training. Three other experts independently annotate every task in the test set. We retain their individual masks and derive two consensus masks for evaluation. Researchers can use these annotations to train models that predict relevant regions or select from proposed regions.

02 Dataset

Dataset and annotations

One annotator supplies the training masks.
Three experts annotate each task in the test set.

The annotations identify visible support for a composition, including objects, surfaces, background, and negative space.

KU-PCPE adds spatial evidence to the positive composition labels of KU-PCP. Each task pairs one image with one applicable label. Annotators select the regions that jointly support that composition.

The three experts worked independently on the test set. They could select, split, add to, or refine proposed regions. Their individual masks preserve different interpretations. We combine these masks in two ways to evaluate models.

How do the two consensus masks differ?+

Strict: a pixel belongs to the target when at least two of the three experts selected it.

Ambiguity-aware: we compare the second and third experts’ masks with the first expert’s mask. If selecting the opposite region gives better agreement, we use that selection instead. A majority vote then combines the three masks. This accommodates choices such as sky or ground around the same horizon.

The annotations reflect subjective judgments, and the proposed regions may influence those judgments. An independent audit without proposed regions has not been performed.

03 Evaluation

Benchmark results

Compare predicted regions with human evidence.
All means are computed over the 1,327 tasks in the test set.

Models and reference scores

The benchmark measures how well predicted regions agree with the experts. Models receive a photograph and its composition label. Oracles also use the target mask to estimate how much of it can be recovered from the proposed regions.

SegFormer-B0 reaches .664 Dice, compared with .395 for the strongest vision-language model (VLM) tested. In a separate comparison, the template method and VLM choose from the same 12 proposals. This tests their ability to select regions while keeping the available proposals fixed.

Same 12 proposals.402 vs. .395

The template method scores .402 and Qwen3-VL-32B scores .395 Dice. The confidence interval for their difference includes zero, so these results do not establish an advantage for either method.
